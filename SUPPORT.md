# Getting help

Most repositories of this organization are private: our products and client work. Here is where to go.

- **A problem with one of our public projects?** Read its README, search its issues, then open an issue with the _Bug report_ or _Feature request_ form.
- **Open source projects** (MakiSend and the others) are in [lixalistudio-oss](https://github.com/lixalistudio-oss): ask there.
- **Itaja**: see [getitaja.com](https://getitaja.com) and the [itaja](https://github.com/itaja) organization.
- **Security problem?** Do not open an issue. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else** (a project, a partnership, press): the [contact form](https://lixalistudio.tech/en/contact).

We never need your secrets: do not post API keys, passwords or encryption keys anywhere.
