## What we build

**[Itaja](https://getitaja.com)** lets you get paid in Africa without a local company: mobile money, cards and wallets, subscriptions, taxes and payouts. Its SDKs and agent skills live in the [itaja](https://github.com/itaja) organization.

**[MakiSend](https://github.com/lixalistudio-oss/makisend)** is a self-hosted transactional email API that runs on your own Cloudflare account. It's open source, like every project in [lixalistudio-oss](https://github.com/lixalistudio-oss).

**[Oh My Hermes](https://github.com/lixalistudio/oh-my-hermes)** is the workflow layer we use to build, ship and run apps with Hermes Agent.

Most of this organization's repositories are private: they hold our products and client work.
